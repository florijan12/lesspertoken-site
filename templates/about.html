About {{ site.brand }}

{{ site.brand }} compares what the same AI models cost through different API gateways, checks the prices every day, and publishes audits of real projects that show where tokens get wasted. It's a small independent site run by {% if site.author.url %}{{ site.author.name }}{% else %}{{ site.author.name or site.brand }}{% endif %}, who also maintains the open-source llm-cost-audit skill. It is not part of SayGM, OpenRouter or any model maker.

How we check prices

  • Every day, automatically. A scheduled job reads SayGM's public model list and OpenRouter's per-provider price list for each of the {{ n_models }} models we track, then rebuilds the site. Every page shows the date of the last update.
  • OpenRouter at its cheapest. For open models, where several providers compete, we take the cheapest provider that is currently up, and we add OpenRouter's 5.5% card fee because that's what you pay when you top up by card.
  • Big moves are held, not guessed. If a price moves by more than 60% in a day, the job keeps the last confirmed price until someone checks it, and the page says when that price was last confirmed.
  • List prices from the makers. Anthropic, OpenAI and Google prices come from their own pricing pages, linked at the bottom of every page.
  • When OpenRouter is cheaper, we say so. Model pages flip their headline and verdict when SayGM isn't the cheaper option, and the price table marks it.

How we earn money

SayGM gives us a reward when someone signs up through our links and adds credit. Your price is the same either way, and we don't take payments from anyone to change a ranking or a verdict. We have no deal with OpenRouter, Anthropic, OpenAI or Google. Every page carries this disclosure at the top.

That reward is why we can keep the price checks, the audits and the free cost audit skill free. The skill is MIT licensed and runs on your own machine.
