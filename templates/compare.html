How SayGM handles your prompts

SayGM's gateway runs inside an Intel TDX enclave, where SayGM can't read your prompts. They stay sealed from SayGM and the host machine until they reach the model provider. An attestation quote shows the gateway is running on genuine Intel TDX hardware.

How far the seal reaches depends on the model you pick. SayGM's models list tells your code which route each model takes, so you can choose before you send.

{{ mx.routes() }}

No prompts in logs

Prompts and completions stay out of every record SayGM keeps. Diagnostics such as latency, retries and error types are kept for 30 days.

Optional guardrails

Switch on guardrails for a key and the personal data or secrets they recognise are replaced inside the enclave before the model sees them. They match the patterns they scan for, so treat them as an extra layer, not a guarantee.

What attestation covers

It shows the gateway runs on genuine Intel TDX hardware with an approved SayGM image. It doesn't cover what a model provider does after the hand-off, and SayGM says its lower-level verification tools are still being prepared for general use.

Good to know before you switch

Market pricing, capped at list

Providers compete for every request, so prices follow the market but never go above the model maker's list price. The date at the top shows our latest check.

Pick your privacy level

Claude, GPT and Gemini requests are sealed through SayGM, then processed by the model provider under its API terms, as with any gateway. For data that must stay sealed end to end, choose a -tee model.

Easy to try alongside OpenRouter

74 models, pay as you go, no subscription. It uses the same API format, so you can keep your OpenRouter key as a ready fallback.
